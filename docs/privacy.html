Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Run by Zero Touch (zerotouch.com.co) · Contact: tasin9209@gmail.com

This page explains what information we collect when you use RUET Café, and what we do with it.

What we get from Google

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID. We never see your Google password.

What we store

  • Your credit balance, plus your recharges, purchases, refunds and orders.
  • Your login sessions, including your IP address and browser details.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To manage your credits and show your purchase history.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We don't share it with anyone, except when the law requires it, and, once online payments are available, with our payment provider so they can process your payments.

How we protect it

Your data is stored on our own server and sent over HTTPS. We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use ads or trackers.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active, and for as long as we need it for our records. To ask us to delete your data, email tasin9209@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
